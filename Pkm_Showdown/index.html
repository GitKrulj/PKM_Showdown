Details

Pokémon Showdown Tournament is an online competition for Pokémon fans who enjoy strategic battles. Players will compete against each other using
their own Pokémon teams. The tournament takes place online through Pokémon Showdown, with the goal of winning matches and reaching the final.

Players will compete against each other using their own teams of 6 Pokémon in exciting Double Battles.

Each matchup will be played as a Best of 3, meaning the first player to win two battles advances to the next round.
